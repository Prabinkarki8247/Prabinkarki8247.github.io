A little about me

Connecting evidence, policy, and practice.

My work brings together applied epidemiology, public health programmes, and research.

I am a public health officer at the Epidemiology and Disease Control Division, Department of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Population, Government of Nepal. My work covers national policy and guideline drafting, surveillance, rapid response training, and coordination with federal, provincial, and local authorities.

My research focuses on aging epidemiology. I hold an MPH with Distinction from the Institute of Medicine, Tribhuvan University, and I review manuscripts for several public health and geriatrics journals.
